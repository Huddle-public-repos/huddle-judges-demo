# Hackathon Judge Scoring Sheet Tool · prototype

A one-screen judge scoring sheet. Pick a team on the left, rate it 1–10 on Innovation & Creativity, Technical Execution, and Impact & Usefulness, and the total (out of 30) updates live with a progress bar. Click a selected score again to unset it. Fully rated teams appear in a ranked leaderboard on the right. The research only confirmed that these are common hackathon rubric themes, so the criteria and equal weighting are assumptions to confirm with Kishore. Scores are held in memory only.

**Built for:** Research how hackathon judges typically score demos and evaluation criteria, then build a simple scoring sheet that allows a judge to rate a team on three criteria (to be determined from research) with automatic total calculation.
